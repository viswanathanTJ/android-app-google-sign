Privacy Policy

Money Manager for Android (com.viswa2k.moneymanager) · Effective 20 October 2026

Summary

Money Manager is a personal finance app. Your financial data stays on your device. The app has no server of its own and does not collect, sell or share your data. The only time data leaves your device is when you choose to back it up, for example to your own Google Drive.

Data stored on your device

The transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, notes, photos you attach and settings you enter are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent anywhere unless you use one of the backup or export features below.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect it:

  • Google account email: the app reads the email address of the account you choose, only to show which account is connected. It is stored on your device and nowhere else.
  • Google Drive access: the app requests the drive.file permission. With it, the app can only see and change files it created. It cannot read any of your other Drive files.
  • What is uploaded: backup files of your Money Manager data, saved to a folder named MoneyManager in your own Google Drive. Scheduled backups (daily or weekly) run only if you turn them on. Older automatic backups in that folder are removed so that at most 15 are kept.
  • Access tokens issued by Google are kept in memory only while needed, and are never stored or logged by the app.

Data in your Google Drive is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Other features that move data

  • Backup to a file, Excel export and e-mail backup: create a file that you choose where to save or send.
  • PC Manager: when you start it, the app serves your data to a web browser on the same Wi-Fi network until you stop it or leave that screen. You can protect it with a passcode.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No collection of your data by the developer.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Drive at any time from the app (More → Backup → Google Drive).
  • Remove the app's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete backups by removing the MoneyManager folder from your Google Drive.
  • Delete all app data with the app's reset option or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
